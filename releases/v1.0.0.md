# MacMiddleClick 1.0.0

Middle clicks on a Mac trackpad, from the menu bar.

- Tap the trackpad with three fingers, or press it with three fingers, to middle-click at the pointer. A pressed middle click can be dragged.
- Turn tap and press on or off separately, or use four fingers instead of three.
- Works with built-in trackpads and Magic Trackpads, including ones that connect later or after sleep.

Requires macOS 14 or newer. The attached app supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

## Install

```sh
brew install --cask henrikhestnes/tap/macmiddleclick
```

Or download `MacMiddleClick.zip`, unzip it, and move **MacMiddleClick.app** to **Applications**.

On first launch, enable MacMiddleClick in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility**.

This build is signed with a self-signed certificate and has not been notarized by Apple. macOS may block the downloaded app through Gatekeeper. The cask does not disable or bypass Gatekeeper.
